# ☕ Uncommon Cup — Customer Onboarding & Operations Process

> **Role:** Business Analytics & Operations Lead  
> **Company:** Uncommon Cup

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to create a structured operational process for converting a potential customer into a successfully served customer while capturing important operational and customer data.

---

# 1. Customer Onboarding Process

### 🔄 Process Flow

**Discover → Visit/Order → POS/Order Recording → Preparation → Service**

| Step | What is Done | Data / Information Captured | Responsible Role | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Discover** | Customer discovers Uncommon Cup through social media, word-of-mouth, promotions or other channels. | Source/channel, customer interest, product/service interest | Marketing & Service Team | Customer becomes aware of the business |
| **2. Visit/Order** | Customer visits the café or places an order and selects products/services. | Order details, products, quantity, customer requirement | Service Staff | Customer order is confirmed |
| **3. POS/Order Recording** | Staff records the order and generates the bill using the POS/billing system. | Order ID, items, quantity, price, total value, payment mode | Cashier/Service Staff | Accurate order and billing record |
| **4. Preparation** | Kitchen/barista team prepares the order according to the recorded requirements. | Order time, preparation time, items prepared, wastage if any | Kitchen/Barista Team | Product is prepared correctly |
| **5. Service** | Completed product is checked and served to the customer. | Service time, order accuracy, customer issue/feedback | Service Team | Customer receives the correct product/service |

---

# 2. Service-Delivery Process

### 🔄 Process Flow

**Order → POS → Kitchen → Quality Check → Service**

### Objective

To ensure that every customer order is recorded correctly, prepared properly, checked for quality, and served to the customer on time.

| Process Step | What is Done | Responsible Role | Expected Time | Quality Check | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Order** | Customer selects the required food/beverage and places the order. Staff confirms the order details. | Service Staff | 3–5 minutes | Confirm item, quantity and special requirements | Confirmed customer order |
| **2. POS** | Staff enters the order into the POS system and generates the bill. | Cashier / Service Staff | 1–2 minutes | Check item, quantity, price and total amount | Correctly recorded order and bill |
| **3. Kitchen** | Kitchen/barista team receives the POS order and prepares the food or beverage according to the order. | Kitchen / Barista Team | Within overall 15-minute turnaround target | Check preparation, portion and hygiene | Prepared food/beverage |
| **4. Quality Check** | Prepared product is checked before serving to the customer. | Kitchen / Operations / Service Team | 1–2 minutes | Freshness → Temperature → Portion → Taste → Hygiene | Approved order ready for service |
| **5. Service** | The checked order is given to the correct customer and any customer issue is handled. | Service Team | 1–2 minutes | Confirm correct order and customer | Correct product/service delivered |
